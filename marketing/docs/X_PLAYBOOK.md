  of your best tweets, **auto-plug** (auto-reply your offer/link under a tweet
  once it gets traction), content queue, analytics.
- **Content batching:** generate from the FQ knowledge base (`CONTENT_IDEAS.md`,
  `RESEARCH.md`) — the moat. Load the tweet bank into the queue, refill weekly.

**API route (full auto, optional):**
- **X API v2** — Free tier barely writes; **Basic ~$100/mo** (~1.5k posts/mo) is
  enough. A small script can post from a CSV/queue on a cron. (Ask and I'll write it.)

**Bannable — DON'T:**
- Mass auto-DM to new followers, auto-follow/unfollow, automated spam replies,
  coordinated multi-account. X detects and suspends. Keep automation to
  scheduling + recycling + tasteful auto-plug; keep replies human.
