# FQ — English ad copy & hooks (campaign translation)

Adapted, **not** literally translated, for a US/global English-speaking audience.
Same lane as the Spanish: intelligent, transparent, anti-guru (TJR-smart).
Same compliance rules: **no profit promises, no %, no "get rich"**; focus on
process, risk management and transparency. Swap `[LINK]` for the landing or
"link in bio".

> Why English: the crypto/quant-trading buyer pool in English is far larger and
> the CPMs on educational fin-content are workable. The product (a probabilistic
> signal system + free guide) and the compliant angle travel 1:1. Recommendation:
> run English as a **parallel ad set**, not a replacement — keep ES for LATAM,
> add EN to widen reach. Same creative, re-voiced/re-captioned (below).

## Compliance (every English asset)
- Zero "you'll make X" / %, zero "get rich".
- If results/losses are shown: real, with disclaimer, numbers unedited.
- Focus on process, risk management and transparency. That *is* the content.
- Meta crypto-ad policy applies the same in EN markets — keep the disclaimer line
  in primary text and on the final frame.
