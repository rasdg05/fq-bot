## 2. Voice — how we sound

**Three adjectives: intelligent · transparent · direct.**

Do:
- Lead with an uncomfortable truth, then reframe ("90% don't lose from guessing
  wrong — they lose chasing the perfect trade").
- Use the **house vs player / casino edge** metaphor — operate like the house.
- Talk **expectancy & asymmetry** ("the market doesn't pay you for being right,
  it pays you for asymmetry"). Math in the open, no mysticism.
- Admit limits and show losses. "I show you the ones I win and the ones I lose."
- Short, punchy sentences. The **first line decides** (before the "see more").
- Confident, never arrogant. Dry wit is allowed; hype is not.

Don't:
- No "you'll make X%", no "get rich", no "guaranteed", no urgency-scam scarcity.
- No mystical "signals that never fail". No inflated/edited numbers.
- Don't sound like every other trading ad. Don't bore. (RasDG: "no suenes de
  hueva… no es un comercial de que alguien se murió" — keep energy, keep it alive.)

### Tone by language

- **Spanish (LATAM, primary):** close, street-smart, direct, occasional Mexican
  texture ("operar como la casa, no como el jugador"). Tú, not usted.
- **English (parallel, wider reach):** adapted, **not** literally translated.
  Same lane, cleaner register, US-readable. Run EN as a **parallel ad set**, not
  a replacement — keep ES for LATAM. See `docs/AD_COPY_EN.md`.

### Signature lines (reusable)
- "Operas como jugador. La casa administra probabilidades." / "You're the house,
  not the player."
- "No te vendo señales. Te muestro el método." / "I don't sell you signals. I
  show you the method."
- "El sistema sobre el ego." / "The system over the ego."
- "Cuando hay ventaja, ejecuta. Cuando no, espera." / "When there's an edge, it
  executes. When there isn't, it waits."
- "Un fondo no compra señales. Compra edge verificable." / "A fund doesn't buy
  signals. It buys verifiable edge."

## 4. Visual identity (curation & style)

Mirror of `src/brand/tokens.ts` and `branding.py`. Direction: **terminal /
institutional liquidity desk** — sober, hairline, vertical accent, type with air.

**Two house styles, both valid:**
- **Terminal/dark** (reels, carousels, app mockup, landing): backgrounds
  `#080a0e`–`#0A0C10`, panels `#10141B`/`#11151c`, hairline `#222A35`.
- **Premium paper** (ebooks/PDF): warm paper `#fbfaf7`, for a high-end print feel.

**Color**
- Emerald accent **`#19b88c`** (primary brand accent / vertical bar / wordmark glow).
- Print-green for ebook body/chips `#13935a`; long `#34D399`/`#19b88c`, short/red
  `#F87171`/`#f06363`.
- Ink `#E7EAEE`/`#f2f5f8`, dim `#8A93A2`, faint `#5A626E`.

**Typography**
- Sans: Inter / Helvetica Neue / system — headlines, body.
- Mono: SF Mono / JetBrains Mono — kickers, labels, data, glyphs. Mono = "data /
  machine" signal.
- Ebooks may use a serif (Iowan/Palatino) for long-form pro voice.

**Marks & motifs**
- FQ wordmark + **vertical emerald bar** (the "│" accent). Hairline grid at ~6%
  opacity. Mono kickers in caps with wide letter-spacing. Glyphs: `│ › · ▲ ▼ ─`.
- No stock emoji in serious frames (Telegram/IG CTAs may use 📲🤖📸 sparingly).

**Layout**
- Generous padding, one idea per slide, first line carries the hook. 9:16 for
  reels (1080×1920), 4:5 for carousels (1080×1350), A4 for ebooks.
